Tooting Tub

Four animals share one inflatable tub on a narrow canal. There are no paddles. Every push comes from a well-timed fart.

  • Each animal sits on one side and farts outward, shoving the tub the other way. Pickles the pig sits at the back, so her toots send you forward.
  • Spikes guard the canal's corners, and spiky buoys drift in later. One touch and the tub pops. Plain walls just bounce you.
  • Gas refills slowly. Grab beans to refuel everyone, chillies for turbo toots, patch kits to survive one hit, and rubber ducks for points.
  • A crocodile follows you upstream and speeds up the further you go. Keep moving.
